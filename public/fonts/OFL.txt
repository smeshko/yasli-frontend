Sofia Sans
  Designer:  Lettersoup (Botio Nikoltchev, Anna Giedrys) for the City of Sofia
  Source:    https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Sofia+Sans
  Licence:   SIL OFL 1.1
  Why:       Bulgarian Cyrillic is its DEFAULT glyph set. No `locl` feature or
             font-feature-settings hack is needed for <html lang="bg"> to render
             correct Bulgarian letterforms (и→u, п→n, т→m, short г).
  Files:     sofia-sans-latin-normal.woff2
             sofia-sans-cyrillic-normal.woff2

Cormorant Garamond
  Designer:  Christian Thalmann (Catharsis Fonts)
  Source:    https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond
  Licence:   SIL OFL 1.1
  Why:       Display face for headings. Verified to ship Bulgarian Cyrillic
             letterforms. The italic is a separate file because
             `font-synthesis: none` forbids a synthesised oblique.
  Files:     cormorant-garamond-latin-normal.woff2
             cormorant-garamond-cyrillic-normal.woff2
             cormorant-garamond-latin-italic.woff2
             cormorant-garamond-cyrillic-italic.woff2

All six files are the `latin` and `cyrillic` subsets as served by the Google
Fonts CSS API, with `latin-ext`, `cyrillic-ext`, `greek` and `vietnamese`
dropped. Each is a variable font: Sofia Sans covers weights 200-900 and
Cormorant Garamond 400-700.

To refresh, re-request the css2 API with a browser User-Agent and download the
woff2 URLs from the `/* latin */` and `/* cyrillic */` blocks:

  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Sofia+Sans:wght@200..900
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,400..700;1,400..700
